03About

Anthony Mahler

Anthony Mahler has been translating and editing scholarly and art writing since 2015, for academic presses, museums, and art publishers. He received his PhD in Germanic studies from the University of Chicago in 2014, after a BA at the University of Pennsylvania and studies in Berlin, Konstanz, and Tübingen. He has held postdoctoral positions in Tübingen and Zurich and has received research grants from the SNSF, the Mellon Foundation, and the Fulbright Program. He teaches at the Language Center of the University of Basel and has lived in Basel with his family since 2012. He reads closely, cares about every text, and enjoys working with authors.
