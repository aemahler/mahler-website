03About

Anthony Mahler

Anthony Mahler is a translator and editor working from German into English. His translations have been published by academic presses, art publishers and museums, among them Routledge, De Gruyter, Hatje Cantz, Yale University Press and the Fondation Beyeler.

He works mainly on books: full monographs, exhibition catalogues, and essays in art history, art theory, philosophy and literary studies, for authors, editors, museums and galleries who want an English text that reads as carefully as the original.
